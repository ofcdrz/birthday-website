A Letter From The Heart

Happy Birthday,
Meri Pyari Behan ❤️

Meri Pyari Behan, aaj ka din duniya ke liye ek aam din ho sakta hai, lekin mere liye yeh din sabse khaas hai — kyunki aaj hi ke din meri zindagi mein sabse pyaari roshni aayi thi.

Tum meri zindagi ka woh hissa ho jo main kabhi kho nahi sakta. Tumhari hansi mein ek aisi taaqat hai jo mere har dukh ko halka kar deti hai. Jab tum khush hoti ho, toh laga jaise poori duniya khush ho gayi ho.

Main duaa karta hoon ke Allah tumhein har us cheez se nawaze jo tumhare dil ko sukoon de. Tumhari mehnat rang laaye, tumhare khwab poore hon, aur tum hamesha apni shartein khud rakho — kabhi kisi ke liye apni khushi na chhodo.

Chahe zindagi mein kuch bhi ho jaaye, chahe hum kitni bhi door chale jaayein — tum hamesha meri sabse pyaari behen rahogi. Meri har dua mein tum hoge, meri har khushi mein tumhara naam hoga.

Happy Birthday, Meri Pyari Behan. Allah tumhein hamesha apni hifazat mein rakhe, tumhein lambi umar de, aur tumhare chehre ki yeh muskurahat kabhi kam na ho. Ameen. ❤️
